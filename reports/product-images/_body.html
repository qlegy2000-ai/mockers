What Can Be Sorted Automatically

The original product records linked each photo to a specific variant, and a variant knows its garment. Tested against all 1,315 source images:

 ImagesShare
Resolvable — only one garment ever used it73255%
Ambiguous — shared by two or more garments23517%
Orphan — never attached to any variant34826%
Just over half can be placed on the right garment with certainty. The remaining 43% cannot: a quarter were uploaded in bulk and never assigned to anything, and a sixth genuinely appear on more than one garment.

Stage 2 — the remaining 43%

Two ways to deal with the 583 images that cannot be placed automatically.

OptionTimeOutcome
Trim~1 hourKeep only images proven to belong to the garment. Products end with roughly 3–6 correct photos each instead of up to 17 mixed ones. No guesswork.
Review by eye4–6 hoursEvery unidentified image looked at and assigned. Keeps the most photos, but hoodie and sweatshirt are hard to tell apart at thumbnail size, so some judgement calls will be wrong.
Recommended: Stage 1 then Trim. The complaint is wrong garments appearing, not too few photos. A t‑shirt page showing five correct photos reads better than seventeen where half are hoodies.
