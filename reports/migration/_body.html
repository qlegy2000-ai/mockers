Problems Found & Corrected

Several of these were faults in the earlier build that only surfaced when the live data was checked against it. All are fixed.

ProblemCorrection
14 designs were missing entirelyThey had no product type set, so the original export skipped them — including DRUNKIN, JOMO, ROLEX and ANDY. All 14 rebuilt and live.
48 designs had lost a colourPurple was being merged into Beetroot Purple. They are different garments and 51 products sell both. Separated and restored.
38 designs had a colour renamedOffwhite was being turned into White. Corrected to the names the shop actually uses.
Wrong garment photos on 1,599 imagesAn internal name clash put sweatshirt photos onto t‑shirt pages. Removed and replaced.
Design artwork was not the first imageAll 321 products now lead with the design, as intended.
Style switcher missing on 17 designsDesigns sold in exactly two garments were hiding the picker. Fixed.
A size spelled Swestshirt, nine wrong size spellingsNormalised, so they do not become extra garments or sizes.
Collection pages briefly unreachableNew collections were created but not released to the storefront. Caught and released the same session.
