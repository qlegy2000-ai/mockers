The White-Ink Rule

A design printed in white ink disappears on a pale garment — cream Offwhite in winter, pure White in summer. Twelve designs are affected, and each new product of theirs was built without that one colour.

DesignColour droppedProducts

PALESTINEWhite & Offwhite3
PERSONALITY DISORDERWhite & Offwhite3
The catalogue already backed this up: nine of the twelve white‑ink designs have never been sold as a White t‑shirt. The rule was being followed by hand — it is now written down and applied automatically.
A handful of older products still sell a pale colour the artwork cannot carry — NATURAL STUPIDITY, THE ROLLING EYES and NEED WEED on Offwhite, and White t‑shirts for MILLENNIALS, KILL BIC and PALESTINE. These were left alone as agreed. Say the word and they come off.
